# TEMLI legal pack — draft

These files are working templates, not approved legal documents. They must not
be published until every bracketed field is completed and a Russian-qualified
lawyer confirms the operator model, minors workflow, retention periods, and
Telegram/cross-border analysis.

Current technical/legal gap status: `COMPLIANCE_STATUS_2026-09-25.md`.

## Launch blockers

1. Identify the personal-data operator: full legal name/status, registration
   details, address, email, and responsible person.
2. Submit the Roskomnadzor operator notification before processing unless
   counsel documents a specific statutory exception.
3. Approve and publish the personal-data policy, separate consent, user
   agreement, and processing instruction.
4. Implement versioned acceptance records and an in-app withdrawal/deletion
   request channel.
5. Approve the minors/parent workflow and data minimization rules.
6. Approve retention periods for operational data, payment/accounting records,
   logs, generated files, and backups.
7. Confirm processor/subprocessor terms with hosting/storage providers and
   complete a Telegram/cross-border transfer assessment.
8. Remove the old `privacy.html` and `terms.html` from launch consideration:
   they describe a discontinued Google Drive backup flow, not the TEMLI product.
