# TEMLI Privacy Notice

**Draft version:** 2026-10-02.1

**Status:** Product-ready draft; publish only after infrastructure, subprocessor, transfer, and legal-basis details are verified for the launch region.

## 1. Who is responsible

For account, support, security, and service-subscription data, the controller is Individual Entrepreneur Roman Aleksandrovich Vereta, Tax ID 501215537312, Primary State Registration Number 325508100542258, Balashikha, Moscow Region, Russian Federation. Privacy contact: 46rus@mail.ru.

For student and parent records entered by a tutor, the tutor normally decides why and how the information is used and acts as controller. TEMLI processes that information to provide the tutor’s workspace and messaging functions. Exact roles can vary by law and must be confirmed in the Data Processing Agreement used for the launch region.

## 2. Scope

This Notice explains how TEMLI handles personal data in the main Telegram bot, Mini App, branded tutor bots, support process, public website, and demonstration page. The public demo contains fictional data and does not connect to a TEMLI account.

TEMLI accounts are available only to adults acting as independent private tutors. Schools and other organisations are not currently supported. Students and parents do not receive TEMLI accounts; they may only become recipients of optional messages from a tutor’s branded Telegram bot.

## 3. Data we process

### Tutor account data

- Telegram ID, username, display name, and language;
- account eligibility confirmation and its date/version;
- settings, time zone, working hours, currency, and connected-bot configuration;
- subscription, payment-support, and service correspondence data when paid access is enabled;
- security and limited technical metadata, such as timestamps, request status, and release identifiers.

### Student and parent data entered by a tutor

- name or pseudonym;
- Telegram ID or username and contact details, where provided;
- parent name and contact details;
- schedule, lesson links, lesson price, payment status, and attendance-related information;
- tutor notes that must not contain sensitive or special-category data;
- branded-bot connection and notification preferences.

### Data we intentionally do not require

TEMLI does not require medical records, biometrics, government identity documents, precise location, political or religious views, or information about race, ethnicity, or sexual life. Tutors must not enter this information.

## 4. Sources

Tutor account data comes from Telegram and the tutor. Student and parent data normally comes from the tutor. A student or parent provides limited Telegram profile and chat identifiers when they voluntarily start the tutor’s branded bot.

## 5. Purposes and legal bases

Depending on the jurisdiction, we process tutor data to:

- create and operate an account and provide requested features — performance of a contract;
- authenticate requests, prevent abuse, secure data, diagnose failures, and protect legal claims — legitimate interests and legal obligations;
- provide support and respond to privacy requests — contract, legitimate interests, and legal obligations;
- bill for paid access and keep required transaction records — contract and legal obligations;
- send product marketing only where permitted, with any required consent and an opt-out.

Student and parent data is processed on the tutor’s documented instructions to provide scheduling, record-keeping, and optional notifications. The tutor is responsible for selecting a valid legal basis and meeting notice or consent requirements.

## 6. Children

A child cannot create a TEMLI account. A tutor may keep a minimal student record and may enable lesson notifications through the tutor’s branded bot. This still involves personal data. The tutor must have the authority required by the child’s jurisdiction and must involve a parent or guardian where required.

For the United States, an online identifier that allows direct contact may be personal information under COPPA. Before commercial use involving a child under 13, TEMLI and the tutor must determine whether direct notice and verifiable parental consent are required. For the EU/EEA, the lawful basis, transparency information, and any parental-authorisation requirements must be assessed under the law applicable to the child.

## 7. Recipients and service providers

Current or planned providers include:

- hosting and storage provider used for the selected TEMLI deployment region;
- Telegram, for authentication, Mini App delivery, bot connections, and messages;
- a network proxy used solely to transport encrypted Telegram Bot API traffic where direct connectivity is unavailable;
- professional advisers and authorities where disclosure is legally required;
- payment providers when paid subscriptions are activated.

We do not sell personal data and do not use student data for behavioural advertising.

Before paid international launch, TEMLI will publish a current subprocessor list identifying the provider, purpose, and processing region.

## 8. International transfers

Telegram and infrastructure providers may process data in countries outside the tutor’s or recipient’s country. The current production candidate may use infrastructure in the Russian Federation and an Estonian proxy for encrypted transport to the Telegram Bot API. The proxy is not used for the database, files, Mini App, or backups, but may process connection metadata.

Before offering paid services in the EU/EEA or the United Kingdom, TEMLI will document the applicable transfer mechanism, transfer impact assessment, supplementary safeguards, and controller approval of subprocessors. If an appropriate transfer cannot be established, the relevant data flow must not be launched in that region.

## 9. Retention

- active tutor, student, schedule, and settings data: while the account is active and needed to provide the service;
- deleted student profiles: removed from the live system promptly after confirmed deletion; residual backup copies expire within the documented backup cycle;
- deleted accounts: live data removed or de-identified after verification, except information retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, or disputes;
- payment and accounting records: for the period required by applicable tax and accounting law;
- security logs: for a limited period appropriate to investigation and service protection;
- branded-bot tokens: until the tutor disconnects the bot or deletes the account.

The final retention schedule and exact backup expiry period will be published before paid launch.

## 10. Security

Measures include Telegram Web App request validation, tenant isolation, access controls, TLS, encryption of branded-bot tokens, secret separation, rate limiting, integrity checks, backup verification, and tests of storage recovery. No system can be guaranteed completely secure; confirmed incidents are handled under the incident-response procedure and applicable notification law.

## 11. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent, and complaint to a supervisory authority. Tutors can export account data and request deletion in the app. Other requests may be sent to 46rus@mail.ru.

We may need to verify identity and authority before acting on a request. A parent or guardian requesting information about a child may need to demonstrate authority without providing unnecessary identity documents. We normally respond within the period required by applicable law; under the GDPR this is generally one month.

## 12. Automated decisions

TEMLI does not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about tutors, students, or parents solely through automated processing.
