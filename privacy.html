Student and parent records may include a name or pseudonym, contact details, Telegram identifiers, parent details, schedule, lesson links, price and payment status, tutor notes without sensitive data, and notification preferences.

Tutor data comes from Telegram and the tutor. Student and parent data usually comes from the tutor; recipients provide limited Telegram identifiers when they start a branded bot. 5. Children
