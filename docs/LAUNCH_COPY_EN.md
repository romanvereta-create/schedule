# TEMLI launch copy

## Positioning

**Category**

Telegram-first workspace for independent tutors.

**One-line value proposition**

Plan lessons, track payments, and keep every student detail in one calm workspace inside Telegram.

**Ideal customer**

An adult independent tutor who manages their own students and currently combines a calendar, notes, spreadsheets, and chat reminders.

**Not supported yet**

Schools, tutoring centres, teams, shared staff accounts, and organisation-wide student management.

## Short sales copy

TEMLI brings your schedule, student profiles, payment status, lesson links, and optional reminders together in Telegram. Move lessons in seconds, see what has been paid, and keep the details you need without running a school-sized system.

## Beta invitation

**Headline**

Spend less time managing lessons.

**Message**

TEMLI is opening a limited beta for independent tutors. Add your students, plan individual or group lessons, track payment status, and send optional reminders through your own branded Telegram bot. The beta is designed for tutors working on their own, not schools or teams.

**Call to action**

Open the demo, then start your TEMLI workspace in Telegram.

## Directory listing

**Name**

TEMLI

**Short description**

Lesson scheduling, student details, and payment tracking for independent tutors in Telegram.

**Long description**

TEMLI is a focused Telegram workspace for independent tutors. Plan recurring individual and group lessons, move sessions with drag and drop, track paid and outstanding lessons, keep student contacts and links together, and export your account data. Student and parent notifications are optional and are sent only through the tutor’s own branded bot. TEMLI accounts are for adult independent tutors; school and organisation accounts are not supported yet.

## Core proof points

- Individual, group, recurring, and personal calendar entries.
- Student profiles with contacts, rates, links, notes, and history.
- Paid status, outstanding balances, and package allocation.
- Optional student and parent messages through the tutor’s branded bot.
- Portable account export and an in-app deletion request.
- A public view-only demo made entirely from fictional data.

## Claims to avoid before verification

- Do not claim that TEMLI is “GDPR compliant”, “COPPA compliant”, or legally certified.
- Do not promise uninterrupted availability or instant permanent deletion from every backup.
- Do not describe payment-status tracking as payment processing, accounting, tax reporting, or a fiscal receipt service.
- Do not market the current version to schools, organisations, or shared teams.
